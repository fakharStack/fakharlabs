## Keyword Validation Notes

- All keywords based on Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, and typical Pakistan market search patterns
- Priority: low-difficulty, high-intent long-tail terms first
- Local variants (city names) should have unique content per page — NOT copy-paste with city swapped (doorway spam)
- Volume validation with Google Search Console Queries report recommended after 4 weeks of indexing
